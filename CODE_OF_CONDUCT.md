# Community code of conduct

[简体中文](./CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

We want participation in Oh My DSH to be safe, constructive, and welcoming.

- Treat other participants with respect.
- Discuss technical decisions on their evidence and trade-offs.
- Do not publish private information, credentials, or confidential material.
- Do not harass, threaten, discriminate against, or impersonate others.
- Respect project and upstream licensing, attribution, and trademark boundaries.

Maintainers may edit or remove contributions and restrict participation when
necessary to protect the community or the project. Report sensitive conduct
concerns through a private repository channel rather than a public issue.
